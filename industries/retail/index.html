BizTrack Pro for Retail Businesses

Retail businesses deal with a repeating set of problems — stock visibility, customer credit, and reconciling Mobile Money payments. Here's how BizTrack Pro's existing features address each one.

The problems retail businesses actually deal with

Every retail shop is a little different, but the operating problems tend to repeat: knowing what's actually selling versus what's sitting on the shelf, keeping stock counts accurate as items move in and out, remembering who bought on credit, and figuring out at the end of the week whether the business made money or just moved money.

Sales and stock visibility

When a sale is logged in BizTrack Pro and marked paid, the item is deducted from stock automatically. That means your stock count reflects what's actually happened at the counter, not what you last counted by hand. Fast-moving items become easy to spot because you can see what's selling in real time, rather than noticing only when a shelf runs empty.

Customer debts

Retail businesses that extend credit to regular customers often track it by memory or in a notebook that can be misplaced. BizTrack Pro records what each customer owes and can send automatic WhatsApp reminders, so following up doesn't depend on you remembering to do it.

Mobile Money reconciliation

If customers pay by MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, or AirtelTigo Money, BizTrack Pro reconciles those payments against your recorded sales, instead of you matching transaction alerts to sales by hand at the end of the day.

Staff and daily performance

If you have staff logging sales, BizTrack Pro gives them access to do that without giving them access to everything — and gives you visibility into what was sold and when, even when you're not the one at the counter.
