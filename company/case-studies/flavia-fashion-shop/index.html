How Flavia Fashion Shop Uses BizTrack Pro to Stay Ahead of Stockouts

Flavia Fashion Shop uses BizTrack Pro's stock reports to know what's running low before heading to market to restock — and to see which products actually sell and which are most profitable.

Written by: BizTrack Pro Team · Reviewed by: BizTrack Pro Team · Last updated: August 2026

CustomerFlavia Fashion Shop
Business typeFashion retailer

The challenge

Before BizTrack Pro, stock running out was something Madam Flavia would only discover after the fact — there was no way to see it coming, which made restocking trips reactive rather than planned.

The solution

Flavia Fashion Shop now uses BizTrack Pro's stock reports to see what's running low before heading to market, instead of finding out too late. The same reporting also shows which products are the shop's best sellers, and which are actually the most profitable — not always the same thing.

The result

Restocking trips are now planned around what the reports show is actually low, rather than guesswork. Madam Flavia also has visibility into which products are worth prioritizing, both by how well they sell and by what they actually earn.

Customer testimonial

“My stock used to run out and I wouldn’t even know. Now, with BizTrack Pro, I go to the market with my stock report showing me what is running low. I also know my best-selling products and which products are most profitable.”

— Madam Flavia, Flavia Fashion Shop

What this shows

Flavia Fashion Shop's experience is a practical example of stock reporting in use: catching low stock before it becomes a stockout, and using real sales and profit data — not just instinct — to decide what's actually worth restocking.
