How Solomon Hardware Store Uses BizTrack Pro to Track Customer Debt and Expenses

Solomon Hardware Store, a construction tools and equipment retailer, has used BizTrack Pro since July 2026 to keep customer debt organized and get a clearer view of the business.

Written by: BizTrack Pro Team · Reviewed by: BizTrack Pro Team · Last updated: August 2026

CustomerSolomon Hardware Store
Business typeConstruction tools and equipment retailer
Using BizTrack Pro sinceJuly 2026

The challenge

Solomon Hardware Store sells construction tools and equipment, often to customers buying on credit. Before using BizTrack Pro, remembering who owed what and staying on top of outstanding debts was difficult to manage — and keeping a clear picture of the business's finances more broadly was its own separate challenge.

The solution

Solomon Hardware Store now uses BizTrack Pro's customer debt tracking and WhatsApp debt reminders to record outstanding balances and follow up on them, instead of relying on a notebook and memory. The business also uses BizTrack Pro to record its expenses, giving a clearer view of where money is going alongside sales and stock.

The result

After about a month of use, Solomon Hardware Store can track customer debts more easily, keep better records of outstanding balances, and use reminders to stay on top of payments. The owner has also described gaining a clearer sense of what running the business is actually costing — in his own words, below.

Customer testimonial

“I never knew how much my business expenses were costing me until Joseph introduced me to BizTrackPro. Now, I can track customer debt on my phone without having to go back to my notebook.”
