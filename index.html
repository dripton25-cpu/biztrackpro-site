The questions every shop owner asks first

"I'm not good with phones — will I mess it up?"
If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can use BizTrack Pro. There's nothing to install, no settings to configure — you just start typing your first sale.
"What if my phone gets lost or stolen?"
Your data lives safely online, not on your phone. Lose the phone, sign in on a new one — every sale, every customer, everything is exactly where you left it.
"What if my staff make a mistake — will I even know?"
Every staff account is separate, with its own login. You can see exactly who recorded what, and when — nothing happens in your business without your name on it.
"This sounds complicated to set up."
There's no training and no manual to read first. You can record your very first sale within two minutes of signing up — the whole app is built to work the way you already think.
"What happens if I stop paying — do I lose everything?"
No card is needed for the first 60 days at all. If you decide it's not for you, you simply stop — nothing is charged, and nothing is taken from you.
"Does this actually work with MTN or Telecel?"
Yes — built specifically for Ghana, with MTN, Telecel, and AirtelTigo Mobile Money support, and pricing in cedis, not dollars.
