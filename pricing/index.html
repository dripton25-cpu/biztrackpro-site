Start with a free 60-day trial

Every new account starts with a 60-day free trial. No credit card is required to begin — you can try BizTrack Pro with your real sales and stock before paying anything.

What's included

There is one plan. Sales, stock, finance, customer debt tracking, and staff access are all part of it — BizTrack Pro doesn't hold features back behind a higher tier.

How payment works

Subscriptions are paid through Paystack, which supports Mobile Money and card payments. You choose monthly or annual billing when your trial ends.

Cancelling

You're not locked into a long-term contract. You can stop your subscription at any time; you'll keep access for the period you've already paid for.

After your trial ends

Once your 60-day trial is over, you'll need to choose the monthly or annual plan to keep using BizTrack Pro. If you're not ready yet, you can wait until you are — there's no pressure to decide before your trial ends.
