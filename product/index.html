What Is BizTrack Pro?

BizTrack Pro is business management software built to connect the parts of a small business that are usually kept apart — so a single sale updates your stock, your finances, and your customer records at the same time.

A connected system, not another app to check

Most small businesses in Ghana run on a mix of a notebook, memory, and a few disconnected phone apps — one for sales, maybe another for stock, and a mental tally of who still owes what. Each piece works on its own, but nothing talks to anything else. At the end of the day, the owner is the one stitching it all together by hand.

BizTrack Pro is business management software that connects the parts of a small business that are usually kept apart: sales, stock, finance, and customers. When one thing happens, the rest of the system updates with it — instead of you updating each part separately.

The problem it solves

When sales, stock, and money live in separate places, a business owner never really knows their business position in the moment. Stock counts drift from what's actually on the shelf. Mobile Money payments get reconciled by memory at the end of the day. Customer debts live in someone's head, or a notebook that can be lost.

BizTrack Pro exists so that recording a sale is also the moment your stock, your finances, and your customer's balance all update — without you doing that work three separate times.

What BizTrack Pro connects

  • Sales — every order you log becomes the source of truth for stock and finance, with invoices generated from the same record.
  • Stock — inventory that updates automatically when a sale is marked paid, with a receipt scanner to add new stock quickly.
  • Finance — sales reconcile against MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, and AirtelTigo Money, so you're not matching payments by hand.
  • Customers — debt tracking with automatic WhatsApp reminders, and VIP tiers for your best repeat customers.
  • Suppliers — keep track of who you buy stock from alongside what you sell.
  • Expenses — record what it costs to run the business, alongside your sales and stock, instead of tracking it separately.
  • Reports — automated reports pull from your sales, stock, and finance records, instead of you assembling them by hand.
  • AI assistant — a chat-based assistant that reads your real sales, stock, and finance data to answer questions about your business, with offline fallback responses when you don't have a connection.
  • Staff — role-based access lets your team log sales without giving everyone full access to the business.

How the system works, in practice

You log a sale from your phone. When it's marked as paid, BizTrack Pro deducts the items from your stock automatically and records the payment against your finances. If a customer bought on credit, their balance updates and BizTrack Pro can follow up with them over WhatsApp. Nothing needs to be re-entered anywhere else.

Who it's for

BizTrack Pro is built for small business owners in Ghana who are currently running their operation without a connected system — shop owners, retailers, and similar businesses who want to see their stock, sales, and money in one place instead of three.

How it's different from a notebook or a spreadsheet

A notebook or spreadsheet can record what happened. It can't update itself. BizTrack Pro's advantage isn't that it's digital — it's that recording one thing (a sale) automatically updates everything connected to it (stock, finance, customer balance), so you're not doing the same bookkeeping work in three different places.

Where it fits into your day

BizTrack Pro runs from your phone, so it fits into the shop day as it already happens — log the sale as it happens at the counter, and let the system carry that information to the rest of your business, rather than reconstructing your day from memory at closing time.

Security and access

Your data is stored in the cloud and syncs as you work, so you're not relying on a single phone as your only copy of your business records. If you give staff access, role-based permissions mean they see what their job requires — not your full financial picture.
